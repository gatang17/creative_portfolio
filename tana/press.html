TANA

Louisville

Opening: July

After first announcing the opening in late 2024, chef Ming Pu is finally opening his much-anticipated Taiwanese American restaurant in Louisville’s Germantown neighborhood. TANA (an acronym for “Taiwanese New American”) celebrates the food of Pu’s native Taiwan while incorporating influence from his culinary career in the American South.
